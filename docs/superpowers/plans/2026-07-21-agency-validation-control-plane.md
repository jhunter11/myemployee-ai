# Agency Validation and Jarvis Control-Plane Plan

**Retained as the agency subplan beneath the Personal Jarvis plan.**

## Problem Statement

Small businesses need trustworthy answers before pricing or billing decisions, not broad AI agents. The agency lacks a client-showable proof, complete commercial/data lifecycle, and safe operator control plane. Demand is unproven: no buyer has supplied files or paid.

## Proposed Solution

Create one end-to-end **Retainer Margin Reset** example. A fictional agency supplies minimized time, invoice, cost, and retainer files. The client sees one margin exception, its source receipts, action scenarios, unmatched rows, and a clean-result path—not agent choreography.

The same workflow becomes the operating system: qualification, reviewed offer, signed scope/data terms, payment evidence, tenant creation, quarantined intake, preflight, reconciliation, independent verification, handoff, decision recording, support, and verified deletion.

Jarvis is a thin conversational manager over versioned workflows, tenant-bound queues, exact workers, approvals, and redacted dashboards. It may explain, draft, validate, execute approved deterministic work, and block unsafe transitions. It cannot send, select tenants from prompts, accept credentials, invent findings, authorize money, release work, or delete data without exact operator approval.

## Assumptions & Bets

We assume:

- Split-stack agencies can export bounded files within 30 minutes.
- Existing tenant, queue, supervisor, audit, and dashboard foundations are reusable.
- Identity, contracts, banking, bookkeeping, and relationships remain human responsibilities.

We bet that reproducible evidence sells better than an “AI agent,” and its intake-to-deletion spine can support later playbooks.

Identity attachment risk: building an impressive autonomous Jarvis or platform before buyers validate the audit.

## Thinking Level Declaration

Level 3: manual consulting or vertical SaaS could work. The Level 4 insight is to verticalize evidence rules while reusing the governed engagement spine. Clean findings are valid; acceptance cannot require bad clients.

## Skill Dependencies

Required: unit economics, accounting, privacy/contracts, secure files, deterministic reconciliation, tenant isolation, evidence design, incidents, and consultative selling. Blockers: commercial identity, approved transfer/payment tools, warm introductions, and professional review.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative                  | Why Rejected                                        |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Generic autonomous agent     | Weak buyer value and unsafe authority.              |
| Recurring integrations first | Demand and non-decaying data access are unproven.   |
| Client portal/cloud platform | Adds procurement and security before first revenue. |

## Quadrant Coverage

| Quadrant         | Plan Element                                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Individual Outer | Demo, offer, CLI, audit, evidence and deletion artifacts             |
| Individual Inner | Domain judgment and honest uncertainty                               |
| Collective Outer | Revenue, client, queue, supervisor, policy and dashboard integration |
| Collective Inner | Operator, buyer, accountant and counsel approval boundaries          |

## Time Horizons

- **V1:** synthetic demo, commercial templates, engagement/artifact/approval records, operator CLI, one adapter, verifier, handoff builder, dashboard projections, and manual paid audit.
- **V2:** after two paid audits and one buyer action, add one demanded connector, bounded model assistance, monitoring, and complete suspend/export/restore.
- **Explicitly not planned:** autonomous outreach, pricing, payments, customer actions, public portal, model-controlled math, multi-vertical platform, or production SLA.
