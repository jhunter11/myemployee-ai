# Outreach Drafts

> **Draft only — no channel is approved for use.** A public contact page is provenance, not consent
> and not proof that the form permits vendor pitches. Jarvis must not send, submit, dial, or schedule
> any of these drafts.

## Mandatory review/send gate

Before one human sends one message:

- Re-open the official site and verify the business, service signal, and channel on the day of use.
- Confirm the channel permits a business inquiry; do not use a homeowner quote form if its terms or
  context exclude vendor outreach.
- Verify the exact recipient or role inbox outside this repository. Store no personal contact data
  in global memory, source control, or telemetry.
- Replace every bracketed field with a truthful fact or delete the sentence.
- Use an accurate sender identity and non-deceptive subject. For commercial email, add the sender's
  valid postal address, clear ad disclosure, and working opt-out process.
- Check applicable federal, state, platform, and site rules. The FTC says CAN-SPAM also applies to
  business-to-business commercial email:
  https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- Do not automate calls or texts. Do not record a call without a separate legal and consent review.
- Record operator approval for the exact channel, recipient, subject, and body. Approval covers one
  send only; editing the copy invalidates it.

## 1. Website contact form

Use only if a current general-business inquiry form expressly allows this kind of message.

**Draft**

> Hi — I run [AGENCY LEGAL NAME] in [LOCATION]. I built a small, rules-based workflow that turns one
> daily lead export into a reviewable qualified-lead brief. Your public site currently shows
> [VERIFIED BUSINESS SIGNAL AND SOURCE URL], so I thought the workflow might be relevant; I have not
> assumed there is a problem. Would the person who owns lead intake be open to a 10-minute synthetic
> demo? The founding first month is $750, then $1,250/month only if it is useful. No CRM writes or
> automated customer contact are included. This is a commercial inquiry. If it is not relevant,
> reply “no” and I will not follow up. — [FULL SENDER IDENTITY]

## 2. Role-inbox email

Use only after a human independently verifies a current public **role inbox** and completes the
commercial-email checklist. The pack intentionally stores no address.

**Subject:** `Rules-based daily lead brief for [BUSINESS LABEL] — 10-minute demo?`

**Draft**

> Hi [BUSINESS LABEL] team,
>
> I’m [FULL NAME] with [AGENCY LEGAL NAME]. I built a bounded workflow that reads one daily lead CSV,
> applies agreed qualification rules, and produces a reviewable brief plus run evidence. I found
> [VERIFIED PUBLIC SIGNAL] on your official site ([SOURCE URL]); that made a short discovery question
> reasonable, but it does not tell me whether you need this.
>
> Does someone currently spend time sorting or merging new estimate requests before the first
> callback? If yes, I can show a 10-row synthetic demo in 10 minutes. The founding first month is
> $750 with no setup fee; continuation is $1,250/month. It does not write to your CRM or contact
> homeowners.
>
> This is a commercial message from [AGENCY LEGAL NAME]. If you do not want another message, reply
> “opt out.”
>
> [FULL SENDER IDENTITY]<br>
> [VALID POSTAL ADDRESS]<br>
> [WORKING OPT-OUT METHOD]

## 3. Manual phone opener

One human may use this only after checking the business number and applicable calling rules. No
autodialer, prerecorded message, synthetic voice, text follow-up, or recording.

**Draft**

> “Hi, this is [FULL NAME] with [AGENCY LEGAL NAME]. This is a brief sales call — is now a bad time?
> I built a rules-based daily lead brief for local roofing teams. It uses a CSV export and does not
> contact homeowners or write back to the CRM. I’m trying to learn whether manual lead sorting is
> actually a problem before I assume there is a fit. Who owns that workflow, and would a 10-minute
> synthetic demo be useful?”

If the answer is no, thank them, record a do-not-contact status, and stop. Do not objection-handle a
clear refusal.

## Follow-up limit

Default to no follow-up. If the operator explicitly approves one and no opt-out or refusal exists,
wait at least five business days and send a two-sentence close-the-loop note through the same
reviewed channel. Never create an autonomous sequence from these drafts.
