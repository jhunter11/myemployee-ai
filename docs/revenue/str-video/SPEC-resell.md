# Version B — Resell the Workflow (Creator Kit) — Specification

> **Status: `spec_only`, and GATED on Version A proof.** Read [README.md](./README.md) first. This
> product markets an earnings-capable method to young creators; it must not ship, and must make no
> earnings claim, until Version A has produced real, documented case studies (see §7). Selling an
> unproven money method — especially to minors — is the fast path to an FTC problem and brand damage.

## 1. Overview

Package the Version-A pipeline as a **productized kit + community** that teaches a young "AI
side-hustle" audience to run the same STR video service themselves: a structured playbook, a prompt +
template pack, tool-setup guides, compliant outreach templates, and an optional community. The
product's honest value is **skill transfer + working templates that compress weeks of trial-and-error
into a weekend** — not a guaranteed income.

We sell the _method and tools_, and we sell it **truthfully**. The niche is full of get-rich-quick
garbage; the differentiation is that ours actually works (proven by A) and is compliant.

## 2. Audience & the minors problem (design-defining constraint)

Target audience: beginner AI creators, skewing young ("teens looking to make AI money"). Two hard
legal realities shape the whole product:

- **Minors generally cannot form binding contracts** and typically **cannot hold Stripe/PayPal
  business accounts** (18+) to collect their own customers' payments. A 15-year-old cannot lawfully
  run the paid pipeline solo in most jurisdictions.
- **Selling to minors** raises consumer-protection and platform-ToS exposure, and refund/chargeback
  risk is higher.

**Resolution (baked into the product):**

- Position for **"students & beginners,"** transact **18+** only at checkout, and provide a
  **parent/guardian pathway** for under-18s (the guardian holds the payment account and co-runs the
  business). This is stated plainly in onboarding, not buried.
- The curriculum teaches the **compliant** pipeline only (reachable buyers, consented assets,
  CAN-SPAM). We never teach scraping hidden contacts or spam — a kit that trains kids to spam is a
  reputational time bomb and drives the exact chargebacks/complaints that kill the STR pipeline.

## 3. Product shape

- **Playbook (core):** the Version-A SOP rewritten as a self-serve course — sourcing reachable
  operators, making a sample, compliant outreach, closing, delivering, upselling a retainer. Modules
  with checklists and "definition of done" per step.
- **Tool kit:** prompt pack, tool-setup guides (which real-estate video tool + exact settings),
  outreach templates (CAN-SPAM fields pre-filled), Stripe + intake templates, the QC checklist, the
  buyer usage guide.
- **Optional light software:** a hosted "sample generator" wrapper so students produce a demo without
  wiring APIs themselves (this is also an upsell / retention hook and a possible recurring-revenue
  tier). Deferred to a later phase.
- **Community (optional tier):** Discord/Skool for accountability, wins, and Q&A.

## 4. Delivery & pricing

- **Platform:** a creator-commerce host (Whop / Gumroad / Skool). No custom billing to build.
- **Tiers (illustrative, validate):** self-serve kit **$49–$99**; kit + community **$149–$199**;
  optional monthly community/software **$29–$49/mo**.
- **Refund policy:** clear, honest, generous enough to defuse "not as described" disputes.

## 5. Go-to-market

- **Top of funnel:** short-form content (TikTok/YouTube/IG) showing the _real_ pipeline and _real_
  case studies from Version A. A free lead magnet (e.g., the sample-generator demo or a mini-guide).
- **Proof-led:** every marketing asset traces to a documented Version-A result. No invented numbers.

## 6. Compliance layer (non-negotiable)

- **FTC earnings claims:** no "make $X/month" promises. Every earnings mention carries a clear,
  conspicuous **income disclaimer** and reflects **typical** results, not cherry-picked outliers.
  Substantiate any figure with real A/beta data.
- **FTC testimonials/reviews rule (2024):** only genuine, verifiable testimonials; disclose material
  connections; no fabricated or incentivized-without-disclosure reviews.
- **Minors:** 18+ to transact; guardian pathway for under-18; no targeting of children.
- **Curriculum compliance:** teaches CAN-SPAM/GDPR-aware, consented, non-scraping outreach only, and
  the same honest positioning as A (marketing content, not Airbnb-conversion guarantees).
- **Platform ToS:** comply with the chosen host's "make money online" / financial-claims policies.
- **IP:** all templates original or properly licensed; no redistribution of third-party tool assets.

## 7. Dependency gate on Version A (why B can't go first)

B may not launch until A has produced:

1. **≥3 real, documented paid deliveries** with buyer permission to reference (anonymized ok).
2. A real earnings/economics readout (so any income language is substantiated, not invented).
3. A working, current tool-setup guide (tools change fast; a stale kit generates refunds).

Until then, B is `spec_only`. Building the _curriculum skeleton and platform_ is fine; **publishing,
pricing, and any earnings claim are gated**.

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk                                    | Mitigation                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FTC / earnings-claim liability          | Income disclaimers, substantiated figures, real testimonials only         |
| Selling to minors (contracts/payments)  | 18+ checkout, guardian pathway, no child targeting                        |
| "Scammy get-rich-quick" brand damage    | Proof-led, compliant curriculum, honest refunds, teach the legit pipeline |
| Students spam/scrape and cause blowback | Curriculum forbids it; teaches compliant sourcing only                    |
| Stale tool guides → refunds             | Versioned kit, update cadence, gated on a current A tool-setup            |
| Market skepticism / saturation          | Differentiate on _real_ proof + compliance, not hype                      |

## 9. Definition of done (kit built, pre-launch)

- Curriculum, prompt/template pack, tool-setup guides, and buyer usage guide authored and internally
  reviewed for compliance (no earnings claims, compliant outreach only).
- Delivery platform configured with refund policy, income disclaimer, 18+/guardian gate.
- A launch checklist that is **blocked** until §7's Version-A proof exists.
- Nothing published, priced live, or marketed with earnings language yet — labeled `built_unverified`.
